# Rift Signal: adult group-home curriculum — Draft 1

Prepared for adults with developmental disabilities in group homes. This is an adult learning and participation resource; no school grade or developmental-age placement is assigned.

## Read in this order

1. [Curriculum rationale and standards crosswalk](01-rationale-and-standards.md)
2. [Staff mission guide: 15 lessons](02-staff-mission-guide.md)
3. [Participant book: readable manuscript](03-participant-book.md)
4. [Participant book: illustrated printable HTML](03-participant-book.html)

For the illustrated book, use GitHub's **Download raw file**, then open the downloaded HTML in a browser. It works offline. Print on Letter paper at 100%, with browser headers and footers disabled. Review the print preview first. It contains 33 planned pages, including two pages per episode; participants can use selected pages over multiple sessions.

## What this draft does

- Grounds the rationale in adult person-directed services, communication rights, direct-support ethics, and accessible learning design.
- Includes optional academic content cross-references without presenting school standards as adult service requirements.
- Connects each staff lesson to actual dialogue cues in the approved disc.
- Gives participants a personal mission book with choices, photographs, drawings, questions, and real-world activities.
- Separates participant ownership from staff observation and service documentation.

## Review status

Content and page counts have been checked against the frozen branch map. This draft has **not** been field-tested, and the HTML has not received a rendered print-layout inspection. Check page fit before printing a full copy. References establish alignment, not certification, clinical efficacy, or eligibility for billing. A provider should map any required local standards separately.

The staff guide explains fixed scanning timing, supported communication, optional participation, and session-only bookmarks. Staff should confirm the listed scene cues on their disc before facilitating. Do not require page completion or a correct answer to continue the story.
