# Rift Signal — My Mission Book

Participant manuscript · Draft 1

This book belongs to me. I choose what goes in it and who may see it.

I can listen, look, speak, point, use my communication system, or ask someone to record my words. I can ask for help, wait, skip, or stop. I do not have to fill every page.

The illustrated version is in [the printable HTML book](03-participant-book.html).
