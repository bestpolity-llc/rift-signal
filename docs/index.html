HOW IT WORKS

Open the kit and run the session.

Rift Signal is built for settings where staff already have enough to do. Each monthly mission arrives with the participant experience and the materials needed to turn it into a structured habilitation activity.

  1. Run the mission. The participant joins the crew as the Asterion's Signal Operator and makes choices that move the story forward.
  2. Use the activity sheet. Staff can extend the mission with communication, sequencing, recall, choice-making, and discussion prompts.
  3. Reflect together. A simple reflection activity gives the participant another way to express preferences, remember events, and talk about the experience.
  4. Document what happened. Staff receive observation prompts and note-writing support to make factual narrative documentation easier.

A real role

The participant is not watching a lesson. They are the Signal Operator, a member of the crew whose choices matter inside the mission.

Built for participation

Missions create opportunities for communication, attention, sequencing, teamwork, recall, decision-making, and self-expression.

Works with ordinary equipment

The DVD format is inexpensive, predictable, offline, and usable on familiar equipment without participant accounts or app-store setup.

FOR DSPs, STAFF & CAREGIVERS

Useful programming without another planning burden.

The goal is not to hand staff another curriculum to design. The mission provides the activity; the companion materials provide practical prompts for participation, discussion, observation, reflection, and documentation.

Documentation support is designed to help staff describe what actually happened: choices made, prompts used, responses observed, communication attempts, participation, recall, and other concrete events from the session.

Rift Signal does not replace a participant's service plan, clinical judgment, agency policy, or required documentation system. Staff use the materials as support and record services in the systems their organization already uses.

PRODUCT BOUNDARY

A structured activity system, not passive entertainment.

Rift Signal is: an accessible simulation, a communication catalyst, a source of meaningful choices, a repeatable habilitation activity, and a staff documentation aid.

Rift Signal is not: a diagnostic tool, clinical treatment, automated billing documentation, a replacement for staff, or a substitute for an individual's formal support plan.
