MISSION 001

First Shift aboard the Asterion

Meet Captain Marcus Vale, mentor Elena Torres, and pilot Chase Mercer. Learn the Signal Operations station, receive an assignment, choose what to observe, transmit your findings, and finish the shift with your crew.

The experience contains 22 connected scenes with narrated character voices and accessible DVD navigation. It is intended to provide an engaging, structured activity—not replace human support.

One switch

Interaction is intentionally simple and built around accessible selection.

Works offline

Burn the ISO to DVD and use it on ordinary DVD equipment without accounts or internet access.

A real role

The participant is the Asterion's Signal Operator: a member of the crew whose choices move the mission forward.

FOR STAFF & CAREGIVERS

Try one shift together.

First Shift is the introduction to Rift Signal. Sit with the participant, allow time for choices, and use the mission as a starting point for communication, anticipation, recall, and conversation.
